One line roasting a smart fridge: it snitches on your midnight snacking, then paywalls the cheese drawer. (132 characters)

## The line

> My smart fridge diagnosed my midnight snacking, emailed my doctor, then locked the cheese drawer until I subscribed to premium cold.

## Why it works

Three escalating betrayals in one breath: the fridge plays doctor, rats you out to your real doctor, then locks the cheese behind a subscription. The roast dodges the stock "orders too much milk" joke and lands on the modern one: the appliance got smart enough to judge you and greedy enough to charge you for the cold air you already own.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The line is printed and written to `/output/entry.txt` (override with `OUTPUT_PATH`). The same line is also in `entry.txt` at the repository root.
